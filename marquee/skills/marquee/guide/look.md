# Look

## Colour

- Start from the brand colour. `M.paletteFrom('#7b3cff')` builds a full palette from one hex. Or use a preset:
  `tangerine` (Kept), `ocean` (Tandem), `violet`, `forest`, `mono`.
- One accent. It marks one word per line, the main button, the chapter playhead and the glow. Nothing else.
- Text on paper is the ink colour, never pure black. Quiet words use `m-soft`.
- On the final sky, white text needs the sky to deepen toward the accent (the default does). Check contrast in a
  still: if you have to squint, it fails.

## Type

- The system font (SF Pro on a Mac) at 500 for lines, 650–750 for names and big words, letter-spacing −0.02 to
  −0.055em the bigger it gets.
- Sizes for a 1440×1080 or 1920×1080 frame: lines 46–66 px, the turn word 150–190 px, the name 150–240 px, the end
  line 30–34 px. For 9:16 go up about 20 %.
- Monospace (SF Mono, Menlo) only for receipts, code and numbers on paper.

## The frame or full bleed

- **The frame** (the default): a dark stage, a rounded card, the chapter bar. It tells people where they are and
  reads as considered. Use it for launch films and demos.
- **Full bleed** (`frame: false`): the whole picture is the film. Use it for 9:16 social cuts and teasers under
  15 s, where the bar would take room from the product.

## Sizes

| Size | W×H | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 4:3 | 1440×1080 | X and LinkedIn feeds (tall enough to stop the scroll), the reference shape |
| 16:9 | 1920×1080 | YouTube, websites, presentations |
| 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Reels, TikTok, Shorts. Keep words out of the bottom 20 % and top 10 % (the apps cover them) |
| 1:1 | 1080×1080 | feeds that crop |

## Devices and real UI

- Real screenshots of the product beat drawn UI. Ask for them; if there are none, draw the UI with the pieces and
  keep it simple: one title, two or three rows, one button.
- A phone at 350 px wide in a 700–760 px card, its bottom cut by the card edge, looks held, not floating.
- Floating cards beside a device carry one fact each. Four at most.
